# Community Code of Conduct

Video Evidence Workbench welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, trade-offs, and reproducible behavior rather than attacking people.
- Give clear, actionable feedback and assume good intent while still naming risk.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, and the sensitivity of source media.
- Credit other people's work and correct attribution mistakes promptly.
- Keep examples safe for a public repository; use synthetic or properly licensed media.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private media or credentials, plagiarism, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately when appropriate, and avoid further exposing affected people.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel when one is available. If the repository has no private channel yet, ask for one in a minimal public issue without naming people or sharing sensitive evidence.
